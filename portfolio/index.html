01

Context

bro.game sells GeForce NOW subscription packages, lets members redeem codes and top up playtime, and runs a referral program that rewards members with stamps. The system unifies these surfaces under one set of tokens and components.

Membership dashboard

Overview, packages, payment, redeem code, profile, play history and devices. Thai-first UI copy.

Referral & rewards

Invite link sharing, stamp milestones, reward unlocks and edge cases for ineligible or maxed-out users.

Marketing site

Public pages on pure black with English copy and full-bleed gaming imagery.

05

Principles

Rules that keep visuals and copy consistent across teams.

One accent

Lime green is the only decorative color. Everything else is near-black, hairline borders and white text.

Glow for emphasis

Emphasis comes from neon glow, not elevation. Regular shadows are reserved for menus and cards.

Verb-first, urgent CTAs

Calls to action are imperative and end with the brand marker “-เลย”, as in สมัครเลย and เริ่มเลย.

Concrete numbers

Show the real count and deadline, as in คงเหลือ 100 จาก 105 ชั่วโมง, never vague scarcity.
